# West coast reverse copies -- 2026-10-01

Approved copy only, word for word, so no line here needs a new approval. Each
section takes an older placed billboard in California, Oregon or Nevada that
faces one way and adds it where the same words are true: on the other
direction of that road, standing before the place, and on the parallel legs
that run the same stretch in either direction and had no sign for that place.
63 signs, nineteen places. Every `leg:` is written the way the driver reads
the sign and `at_mi` counts from that end.

Placement follows the state sheets: projected onto the legs' dense geometry,
"ahead" signs about five to ten miles out, each at least 2.2 miles from every
other callout heard in the same direction and from every sign on the
west-coast sheets. None stands on a designated scenic highway or byway. The
two Wendover Will signs stand on I-80 in Utah, a few miles east of the line.

Not carried over: "The Bakersfield Sound" (its last line says the Crystal
Palace still swings, and the Crystal Palace closed in 2025), "The High
Desert" (true only coming from the Willamette side), "Giant Artichoke"
(written for the approach from San Jose), "The Goonies" (US 101 is a scenic
byway), "Bravo Farms Treehouse" (could not confirm it is still open), and
the Fallon, Ely, Twin Falls and Coeur d'Alene signs, which the Nevada and
Idaho sheets carry onto their other roads.
