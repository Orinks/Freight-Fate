# Indiana attractions, both directions -- 2026-10-01

Draft for the owner's review, not yet approved.

Fourteen real Indiana roadside attractions, each signed in every direction a
leg passes it, 60 signs in all. Every `leg:` is written the way the driver
reads the sign and `at_mi` counts from that end; `tools/bake_billboards.py`
mirrors the milepost onto a leg stored the other way round and records which
way the billboard faces.

Indiana permits billboards beside its Interstates and primary highways in
commercial and industrial areas under an INDOT permit (Indiana Code 8-23-20).
23 USC 131(s) bars new ones on designated byways, so nothing here stands on
the Lincoln Highway byway (US 30 Valparaiso to Fort Wayne, and the original
route through Elkhart and South Bend that US 33 follows), the Historic
Michigan Road (Rochester to South Bend), the Historic National Road (US 40),
Indiana's Historic Pathways (US 50 and US 150, which join US 41 at
Vincennes), or the Ohio River Scenic Byway (INDOT, Indiana Byways; Wikipedia,
"List of Indiana Scenic Byways").

Mileposts come from projecting each attraction onto the legs and reading the
legs' interchange lists. "Next exit" signs stand between the attraction's exit
and the exit before it, one to two miles out unless a callout is in the way;
"ahead" signs about eight to twelve miles out. A sign about a city stands on
the legs into it. Each sign sits at least 2.2 miles from every other callout
heard in the same direction, and from every sign on the 2026-10-01 sheets.

## Indianapolis Motor Speedway Museum

Reopened April 2, 2025 after an $89 million renovation, open daily; the
Winners Gallery holds more than thirty winning cars and the Borg-Warner
Trophy, which carries the sculpted face of every Indianapolis 500 winner
(IndyCar, 2025-04-02; imsmuseum.org; Wikipedia). Signed on the five legs
into Indianapolis from the west, which pass within about three miles of the
track; the other approaches cross the city first.
