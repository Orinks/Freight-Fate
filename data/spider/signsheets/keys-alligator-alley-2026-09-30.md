# The Keys and Alligator Alley carry no billboards -- 2026-09-30

Florida Statutes 335.092 bans advertising signs along Alligator Alley (I-75
between its tollgates), and US 1 from Florida City through the Keys runs the
18-Mile Stretch's conservation land and then the Florida Keys Scenic Highway,
where 23 USC 131(s) bars new billboards and Monroe County's code (section
142-4) prohibits off-premises signs. Owner ruling, 2026-09-30: the Keys signs
keep their copy as roadside landmark callouts, heard as the truck passes
them under the landmark switch, facing the way they always did; on the
Alley, Skunk Ape and Everglades City move west of the Golden Gate toll plaza
(before Exit 101) and the other two go. Robert Is Here, on the mainland in
Homestead, stays a billboard.
