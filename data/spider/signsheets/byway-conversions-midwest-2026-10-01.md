# Midwest byway conversions -- 2026-10-01

Draft for the owner's decision, not yet approved.

Three placed billboards stand on National Scenic Byways, where 23 USC 131(s)
bars new billboards and state law agrees: Kentucky prohibits billboards on a
designated scenic byway (KRS 177.830 to 177.890; Kentucky Transportation
Center), and US 23 through Paintsville and Pikeville is the Country Music
Highway, a National Scenic Byway from Greenup to Jenkins (KYTC list of state
and national byways); US 20 from East Dubuque to Galena is the Great River
Road, a National Scenic Byway (Enjoy Illinois; Wikipedia, "U.S. Route 20 in
Illinois"). Following the 2026-09-30 ruling for the Keys, each becomes a
roadside landmark callout with the same words minus "Billboard:", facing the
way it always did, at the same milepost.
