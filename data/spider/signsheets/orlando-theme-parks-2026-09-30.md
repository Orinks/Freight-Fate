# Orlando theme parks on I-4 -- 2026-09-30

Owner request: one plain sign for each of Walt Disney World, Universal
Orlando, SeaWorld Orlando and LEGOLAND Florida, each way on I-4, naming
what each is and how to reach it, with none of their advertising language.
Positions from the Orlando to Tampa leg's interchanges (Exit 75 for
Universal sits between 77 and 74A; the unnumbered "Sea World" exit; Disney's
Exits 67, 64 and 62; Exit 55 for US 27 to Winter Haven), each at least 2.2
miles from the WonderWorks, Giant Orange, Theme Park Capital and town
callouts heard the same way. Facts: four Disney theme parks and two water
parks (disneyworld.disney.go.com); Universal's third park, Epic Universe,
opened in May 2025; LEGOLAND Florida opened in 2011 on the old Cypress
Gardens grounds, about twenty miles south of Exit 55 by US 27.
