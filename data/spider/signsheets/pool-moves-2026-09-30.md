# Pool lines moved to their places -- 2026-09-30

These seven lines lived in the corridor pools with a state anchor, so a
"next exit" or "ahead" about one real place was read anywhere in the state,
in either direction. Here each is placed on every leg that passes the place,
in each direction its copy holds, with the copy unchanged. Every `leg:` is
written the way the driver reads it; `at_mi` counts from that end.

Mileposts come from projecting each place onto the legs' route geometry and
from the legs' interchange lists. "Next exit" signs stand between the
place's exit and the one before it; "ahead" signs about eight to fifteen
miles out. Each sits at least two miles from any other callout, so the
landmark spacing does not drop it. Places: Cabazon Dinosaurs (I-10 Exit
106, Main Street); Baker (I-15 Exits 246 and 248); Little America (I-80
Exit 68); the Grapevine grade (Grapevine at its foot, Tejon Pass at the
top); Hope (I-30 Exit 30); Muskogee (the US 69 and US 64 approaches);
Casey (I-70 Exit 129).
