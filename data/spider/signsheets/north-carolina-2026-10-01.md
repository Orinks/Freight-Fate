# North Carolina attractions, both directions -- draft 2026-10-01

DRAFT for owner review. Nothing here is approved.

11 real attractions, 40 signs. One more block corrects an existing callout: Fort Liberty was
renamed Fort Bragg in February 2025.

Each is signed in every direction of travel where a leg passes it, except
where a note says why not. Every `leg:` is written the way the driver
reads the sign and `at_mi` counts from that end; `tools/bake_billboards.py`
mirrors the milepost onto a leg stored the other way round and records which
way the billboard faces.

Billboard law: North Carolina permits off-premise boards beside its
Interstates and federal-aid primaries in zoned and unzoned commercial and
industrial areas under its Outdoor Advertising Control Act (G.S. 136-126
to 136-140.1, NCDOT permit, 19A NCAC 2E .0200), and boards line I-85, I-95,
I-40 and I-77 today. Federal law (23 U.S.C. 131(s)) bars new boards on
designated scenic byways; the Blue Ridge Parkway carries none, and nothing
here stands on it. Web search ran out before the state's scenic byway list
could be checked road by road (see the review file); the signs stand on
Interstates and on US 17, US 25, US 52 and US 264, where boards stand.

Mileposts come from projecting each attraction's turnoff onto the legs'
dense route geometry and from the legs' interchange lists. "Next exit"
signs stand between the attraction's exit and the exit before it; "ahead"
signs about eight to fifteen miles out; a sign naming a town stands on the
legs arriving there. Each sits at least 2.2 miles from every other callout
heard in the same direction, including the other east-south sheets.

## Correction: Fort Liberty is Fort Bragg again (Greensboro to Fayetteville)

The existing callout on the Greensboro to Fayetteville leg still says
"Fort Liberty". The post was renamed Fort Bragg on February 14, 2025, now
for Private First Class Roland L. Bragg, a World War II paratrooper
(Wikipedia, "Fort Bragg"). This block keeps the record's name, so a bake
replaces it, and changes only the post's name in the spoken line; it stays
heard both ways, as the existing record is. Its `describe:` notes are kept
in the processed Batch1 sheet.

### Fort Liberty
- treatment: landmark
- leg: greensboro_nc_us -> fayetteville_nc_us
- at_mi: 90.0
- facing: both
- spoken: You are nearing Fayetteville and Fort Bragg, home of the Eighty-Second Airborne and the Green Berets. It is one of the largest military bases on Earth.
