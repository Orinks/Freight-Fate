# Kentucky attractions, both directions -- 2026-10-01

Draft for the owner's review, not yet approved.

Twelve real Kentucky roadside attractions, each signed in every direction a
leg passes it where the callouts allow, 43 signs in all. Every `leg:` is
written the way the driver reads the sign and `at_mi` counts from that end;
`tools/bake_billboards.py` mirrors the milepost onto a leg stored the other
way round and records which way the billboard faces.

Kentucky permits billboards beside its Interstates, parkways and primary
highways in commercial and industrial areas under a Transportation Cabinet
permit (KRS 177.830 to 177.890, 603 KAR 10:040), and no new billboard may go up
on a scenic highway or byway once it is designated (Kentucky Transportation
Center, Highway Knowledge Portal). The state's byways that touch these legs
are US 23 (the Country Music Highway, a National Scenic Byway, Greenup to
Jenkins), US 231 between Hartford and Beaver Dam and US 60 around Owensboro and
Henderson (Blues to Bluegrass), and the Woodlands Trace inside Land Between the
Lakes (KYTC list of state and national scenic byways). No sign here stands on
one; the Land Between the Lakes signs stand on I-24.

The I-65 leg from Indianapolis to Nashville labels its Kentucky exits out of
order (Cave City is listed as Exit 112, Park City as 121); these signs are
placed from the real exits' positions on that leg, matched against the
Elizabethtown to Bowling Green leg. Each sign sits at least 2.2 miles from
every other callout heard in the same direction, and from every sign on the
2026-10-01 sheets.

## The Florence Y'all water tower, Florence (I-71/I-75)

Built in 1974 and painted FLORENCE MALL to advertise a mall that opened in
1976; state highway officials ruled the sign illegal because it advertised
something that did not yet exist, so the city turned MALL into Y'all for $472
(Wikipedia, "Florence Y'all Water Tower"). Southbound out of Cincinnati on I-71
the Covington-suburb callouts leave no room, so that direction is unsigned.
