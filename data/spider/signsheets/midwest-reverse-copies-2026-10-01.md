# Midwest reverse copies -- 2026-10-01

Approved copy reused word for word, no new text. Each block puts an older
placed billboard on a side of its road that had no sign for that place:
the other direction of a one-way sign, or another leg on the same road that
passes the same place in a direction with no sign. Every copy is true from
where it stands; each sign stands before the place in its direction of
travel. Names carry a direction so they never replace the original record on
a shared leg. Every `leg:` is written the way the driver reads the sign and
`at_mi` counts from that end.

Left out on purpose: signs whose copy names the town the leg starts in
(Touchdown Jesus, Terre Haute, the Corvette Sinkhole's own Cumberland Parkway,
Hazard, Pikeville, Van Lear, the Big House, the Motor City, Dayton, Elkhart,
Cincinnati, Traverse City's "you are leaving"), the giant fork near Bowling
Green (its location is not recorded), the New River Gorge Bridge from the
south (US 19 there runs beside the Coal Heritage Trail national byway), and
anything on US 23 in Kentucky (the Country Music Highway, a national byway).
Spacing: 2.2 miles from every callout heard in the same direction and from
every sign on the 2026-10-01 sheets.
