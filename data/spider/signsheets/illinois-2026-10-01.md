# Illinois attractions, both directions -- 2026-10-01

Draft for the owner's review, not yet approved.

Fifteen real Illinois roadside attractions, each signed in every direction a
leg passes it, 67 signs in all. Every `leg:` is written the way the driver
reads the sign and `at_mi` counts from that end; `tools/bake_billboards.py`
mirrors the milepost onto a leg stored the other way round and records which
way the billboard faces.

Illinois allows billboards beside its Interstates and federal-aid primary
highways in business areas under an IDOT permit (Highway Advertising Control
Act of 1971, 225 ILCS 440, https://www.ilga.gov/ftp/ILCS/Ch%200225/Act%200440/022504400F.html).
No sign here stands on a designated scenic byway: the Route Sixty-Six and
Great River Road National Scenic Byways follow the old surface roads, not
Interstate 55 or US 20 east of Galena, and the Illinois River Road (Illinois
29 along the river into Peoria) carries none, so the two Peoria approaches
that run it are left unsigned.

Mileposts come from projecting each attraction onto the legs and reading the
legs' interchange lists. "Next exit" signs stand between the attraction's exit
and the exit before it, one to two miles out unless a callout is in the way;
"ahead" signs about eight to fifteen miles out. A sign about a city stands on
the legs into it. Each sign sits at least 2.2 miles from every other callout
heard in the same direction (rivers, museums, villages within a mile and a
half of the road, billboards facing the same way, and every sign on the
2026-10-01 sheets).

## The Gemini Giant, Wilmington (I-55 Exit 241)

The thirty-foot fiberglass astronaut holding a rocket stood outside the
Launching Pad drive-in on Route 66 from the 1960s until 2024, when the Joliet
Area Historical Museum bought him; he was restored and set up at the entrance
to South Island Park, 201 Bridge Street, Wilmington, on November 29, 2024,
with a visitor center beside him since May 2025 (Wikipedia, "Gemini Giant";
Shaw Local, 2024-12-02 and 2025-04-28). From I-55 Exit 241, Route 44 and
Illinois 53 run into town (Roadside America).

## Lincoln, Illinois (I-55 Exits 133 and 126)

August 27, 1853, he christened it by squeezing the juice of a watermelon onto

10. The Lincoln Heritage Museum callout on the Springfield to Bloomington leg

## Cahokia Mounds State Historic Site, Collinsville (I-55/70 Exit 6)

The largest pre-Columbian city north of Mexico, ten to twenty thousand people
at its peak around 1050 to 1150, larger than London then; Monks Mound, a
hundred feet tall, was raised by hand with earth carried in baskets
(Cahokia Mounds Museum Society; World History Commons). The museum reopened
in June 2026 after four years of repairs, Wednesday to Sunday (St. Louis
Public Radio, 2026-06-10). Exit 6 is Illinois 111. Southbound on Chicago to
St. Louis the leg's Mississippi River callout sits between Exits 9 and 6, so
that sign stands after Exit 11 and reads "ahead".
