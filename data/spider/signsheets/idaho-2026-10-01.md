# Idaho attractions, both directions -- 2026-10-01

Draft for the owner's review, not yet approved.

Eleven real roadside attractions in Idaho, each signed in every direction a
leg passes it, 83 signs in all, plus two sections that carry approved copy
word for word onto the other roads into Twin Falls and Coeur d'Alene (7
signs). Every `leg:` is written the way the driver reads the sign and `at_mi`
counts from that end; `tools/bake_billboards.py` mirrors the milepost onto a
leg stored the other way round and records which way the billboard faces.

Idaho allows billboards beside its Interstates and primary highways under an
Idaho Transportation Department permit (Idaho Code title 40, chapter 19), and
no new sign may go up along a designated scenic byway (ITD; 23 USC 131(s)).
On the game's roads the clear case is Highway 55 from Boise to McCall, the
Payette River Scenic Byway; the International Selkirk Loop runs out of
Sandpoint to the north and west, so no sign stands north of Athol. The Lake
Coeur d'Alene byway is Highway 97, not I-90.

Mileposts come from projecting each attraction onto the legs' dense geometry,
read against each leg's interchange list. "Next exit" signs stand between the
attraction's exit and the exit before it, one to two miles out, and only
where the interchange list has no gap in its exit numbers there; otherwise
the sign reads "ahead". "Ahead" signs stand about eight to twelve miles out.
A sign about a city stands on the legs into it, never on the ones leaving.
Each sign sits at least 2.2 miles from every other callout heard in the same
direction and every other sign on the west-coast sheets.

## Wallace, center of the universe (I-90 Exits 61 and 62)
